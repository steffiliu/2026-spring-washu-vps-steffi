Reading Response: Shifting Website

I found this reading really interesting because it redefines websites as creative, personal spaces. I enjoyed the methaphor comparing a website to a garden or house. It made me think about how websites can constantly evolve and reflect someone’s identity over time. I also liked the argument that individuals and artists should help shape the future of the internet instead of leaving it controlled by large corporations. The idea that a website does not have to be perfect or “finished” was especially meaningful because it makes the process of creating online feel more experimental and personal.
