Welcome to O+O Pizza

We are a New American restaurant inspired by the neighborhood trattorias throughout Italy. Located in the heart of Old Webster, O+O Pizza is a cozy yet sophisticated neighborhood destination, bringing together great food and genuine hospitality in a lively, welcoming space.

Our Menu

We aim to celebrate great times with family and friends, at the table through thin-crust pizzas, pastas and more, along with a thoughtful selection of our favorite wine, beer & cocktails.
