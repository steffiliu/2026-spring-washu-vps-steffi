What is something you appreciate or long for from the past? Why?

Something I appreciate from the past is my old house in Illinois. I moved to New Jersey when I was 10 (on my birthday!), and while I definitely associate myself more with Jersey, I still appreciate the childhood I had in Illinois. They are very different. Naperville, IL, is a pretty big town/city with lots of big neighborhoods and commercial areas. I also feel like it was somewhat diverse racially and socioeconomically. In comparison, in Basking Ridge, NJ, it is very much a smaller, East Coast vibe town. While there are definitely neighborhoods, a lot of houses are further apart and surrounded by lush trees. I also feel like it is less diverse in both senses, but also has a closer community feel. From time to time, I still go on Google Maps’ Street View and see how my favorite childhood spots, like the ice rink I used to figure skate at, have changed. It brings me a lot of nostalgia and reminds me to be grateful for all the different experiences I had in both Illinois and New Jersey.
