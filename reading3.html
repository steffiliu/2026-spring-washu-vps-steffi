Reading Response: Calm Technology

I think the idea of calm technology is really important, especially today when we’re constantly bombarded by notifications and screens. Technology shouldn’t just demand our attention; it should fit naturally into our lives and make us feel in control rather than overwhelmed. I love the examples Weiser and Brown give, like the inner office windows and the Dangling String, because they show that even simple, thoughtful designs can make a big difference in how we experience information. I also find it exciting to imagine a future where computers are everywhere but don’t feel intrusive, letting us focus on what really matters while still staying connected. Overall, I believe designing technology with calmness in mind is essential for creating a more human-centered, balanced world.
