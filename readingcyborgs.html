Reading Response: Cyborgs VS Rooms

Matt Webb’s essay presents two very different visions for the future of technology in computers becoming part of our bodies through wearables and AI, and computers becoming part of the environments around us. I found his idea of “room-scale computing” especially interesting because it focuses on shared, physical spaces instead of isolating people through personal devices. I resonate more with the “rooms” vision because it feels more human-centered and collaborative, allowing technology to exist naturally within everyday life instead of constantly being attached to us. His comparison between “cyborgs” and “rooms” made me think about how technology changes not only what we can do, but also how we interact with others. I also liked the idea of “somaforming,” where environments adapt to humans rather than humans constantly adapting themselves to technology. Overall, the essay made me reconsider what kind of future we are currently building and what role technology should play in our lives.
